# LoopFollow for Android

LoopFollow for Android shows the glucose, loop status and alarms of someone using Loop, Trio or another Nightscout
uploader. It follows [LoopFollow](https://github.com/loopandlearn/LoopFollow) for iOS; the differences are listed in
[DEVIATIONS.md](DEVIATIONS.md).

To build your own copy in GitHub, follow [LoopFollow for Android](https://loopfollowdocs.org/android/lf-android/) in
the LoopFollow documentation. Builds are made with
[LoopFollow Android Builder](https://github.com/loopandlearn/LoopFollowAndroid-Builder) and signed with your own key.

LoopFollow is not a medical device and is not a substitute for advice from your care team.
